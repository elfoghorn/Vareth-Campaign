Requisition Shop

What you bring back, Halda can build from. Phases unlock as your table completes contracts. Spend Requisition Credit (RC) on whatever's open.

Scope Notice This catalogue covers reclaimed field salvage only. Standard manufacturer gear, GMS, IPS-N, SSC, HORUS, Harrison Armory, and anything else on your License page, unlocks the normal way, through License Level and manufacturer rank in COMP/CON. It is not listed here and RC cannot buy it. This ledger is Halda's workshop, not the requisition depot.

OP.01 // ACCESSHow Unlocking Works

Each phase below is keyed to a specific contract. Your GM clicks Unlock Phase once your table completes it, and its items open for purchase. Unlocking a phase doesn't spend RC. It only opens the door. You still need RC on hand to actually take something out of the hangar.

GM Note: Where This State Lives Phase unlocks are saved in this browser only, using local storage. They do not sync between devices. If you open this page on your phone and your player opens it on their laptop, each sees its own unlock state. Treat this as your own reference tool, or narrate unlocks out loud at the table.

RC is earned per pilot, per contract. See the Rules page for the full earning and spending tables. Short version: main contracts pay 4 RC, side contracts pay 2 RC, and clean work pays bonuses on top.
