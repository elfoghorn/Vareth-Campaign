Your Employers

In 2554 there is little difference between a government and a corporation. Paperwork is most of what separates them. This section covers who signs your cheque, and who else may be on the ground.

OP.01 // EMPLOYERAtlas Corporation

Founded2201, Solenne Primus
IndustryPlanetary terraforming and expansion contracting
Motto"Ground Worth Standing On"
ColoursSlate grey and terracotta
ReputationThe careful, professional choice in terraforming
Your DivisionContract Security. Escort. Clearance. Response.

Atlas sells competence. They move into worlds nobody else will touch, make them liveable, and set the price everyone downstream pays for a century. That business model runs on one requirement: getting there first.

Contractors on their second or third Atlas rotation will tell you the pay is reliable. They will also tell you the risk assessments run optimistic. Both statements are true.

Internal Structure: What You'd Know TerraOps runs the actual terraforming and sets your schedule. Contract Security is you, commanded by Capt. Eira Solberg. She is fair and visibly frustrated most of the time. Other divisions exist. You have not been introduced to them.

OP.02 // UNCONFIRMEDWho Else Is Out There

Atlas's official position: KX-19 is an uncontested claim. Contractors who spend time in the field have started to doubt that. None of the following is confirmed. Repeating it around Site Command is a good way to earn a talking-to.

Rumoured Presence

Kestrel Dynamics

A private military corporation. They do not do terraforming. They do war. If they're here, they're not here to make anything liveable. Their pilots publicise kill counts like sports statistics.

Their hardware isn't uniform, either. Some of it's old and brutal, heavy enough to simply refuse to die. Some of it's newer, faster, clearly imported rather than built to any doctrine anyone recognises. Nobody's explained why they're running two completely different philosophies at once.

Rumoured Presence

Verdance Bio-Concern

Conservation and xenobiology. Soft colours, clean press releases, heavy use of the word "stewardship." Nobody in Contract Security has met one directly. Their research stations reportedly pay very well for Bloomlands samples.

Rumoured Presence

The Salt Line

Not a company. A salvage and information network with no registered charter and no fixed base. If they exist, they are already inside somebody's supply chain. Possibly yours.

Whatever's cheapest and most deniable, that's most of what they run. Except for one line. A few of their pilots fly frames from an outfit called Freistride Chassisworks: three people and a mercenary founder, apparently, with every frame named after a dead philosopher. Nobody quite understands why Salt Line runners treat that gear like it means something, when everything else they touch is disposable.

Player Note Your pilot has heard of these organisations the way you've heard of companies that don't operate in your town. Whether any of them are actually on Vareth is not something you know.

OP.03 // CONTEXTWhat "Corporation" Means in 2554

Corpro-states aren't companies in the modern sense. They hold territory, field militaries, issue currency, run courts, and negotiate treaties. Working for one isn't a job. It's closer to citizenship with a termination clause.
