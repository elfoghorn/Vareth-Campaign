Manufacturers

Five names cover most of what you'll see on Vareth. A few more show up on other people's frames. Worth knowing which is which before you're staring down the wrong one.

OP.01 // THE CORE FIVEWhat You'll Actually License Into

Every pilot on Vareth started in one of these. Your License page in COMP/CON progresses through them the normal way. Nothing here is Vareth-specific. It's just the galaxy you're operating in.

Baseline

General Massive Systems

The galactic standard. Reliable, forgettable, universally compatible, and exactly as unglamorous as GMS's whole reputation promises. Everyone starts here. Everyone's still standing on it underneath whatever they've since specialised into.

Escalation

IPS-Northstar

Tough, resilient, built for close combat and for taking a beating without going down. Closely associated with the Albatross, a galaxy-famous peacekeeping force. Atlas leans on this line hard once you're past the basics.

Fieldwork

Smith-Shimano Corpro

Fast, agile, built to scout, spot, and vanish. SSC also does exotic experimental systems nobody fully understands yet, sold with a straight face and a service contract attached. Verdance's preferred supplier.

Esoteric

HORUS

Hacking-focused, and culturally invested in treating non-human intelligence as something closer to a person than a tool. See Rules, OP.02 for what that actually means on this planet specifically.

Premium

Harrison Armory

Imperial, expensive, built around overwhelming firepower and heavy reactors. Runs entire core worlds elsewhere in the galaxy. Rare out here, and rarer still to see fielded by anyone other than the people who can actually afford it.

OP.03 // ON THE MARGINSNobody's, or Everybody's

Unaffiliated

Independent Mercenary Contractors

Heavy, defensive doctrine: layered shielding, area-denial firepower, built to endure rather than outmanoeuvre. Not tied to any of the four corporations. They show up on Vareth's margins following opportunity, sometimes hired by one corp for work too dangerous or too deniable to assign their own people, sometimes just passing through. On a planet this valuable, somebody's always willing to sell violence to the highest bidder.

Player Note None of this is something your pilot can requisition directly. It's what you'll recognise on someone else's frame across a field, in a dossier photo, or in a firefight that's about to go very differently than you expected.
