OP.01 // POSITION SUMMARYWhat You've Signed Up For

Employer

Atlas Corporation

A terraforming megacorp holding first-claim rights to Vareth. Your division: Contract Security. Escort. Clearance. Response. You are contractors, not soldiers. Legal insists on the distinction.

Posting

Site Zero

Atlas's primary dome complex in Ashfall Delta. Roughly 2,500 personnel on rotation. Barracks, hangar, medical, and a bar called the Undertow.

Hazard Classification

Category III

Aggressive native fauna. Residual pre-Diaspora infrastructure. Breather-assist required outside domes. Hazard pay applies.

OP.04 // TONEWhat Kind of Game This Is

Expect

  • Mech combat with real tactical weight
  • A planet that reveals itself slowly
  • Downtime that matters as much as missions
  • NPCs whose lives continue without you
  • Moral choices with no clean answer
  • Corporate employers who are never fully honest

Don't Expect

  • A clear villain to defeat
  • An employer worth trusting
  • Being the good guys
  • Every mystery answered
Table Note This campaign runs on one idea: nobody here is clean. That includes whoever signs your paycheque. Eventually, it includes you. Build a pilot who can be compromised and still be interesting.
