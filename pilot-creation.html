Build a Pilot

Everything you need for session zero. The mechanical part takes twenty minutes. The rest of this page is the part that makes your pilot matter when the campaign starts taking things away from them.

OP.01 // MECHANICSThe Twenty-Minute Version

Use COMP/CON — it's the free official builder and it does all the maths. See the Rules page for setup.

  1. Start at License Level 0. Everyone begins with GMS gear — the standard-issue stuff Atlas hands out. You'll unlock manufacturer licenses through play.
  2. Pick a callsign. This is what people actually call you. Make it something a bored dispatcher would say twenty times a shift.
  3. Assign your four HASE stats (Hull, Agility, Systems, Engineering) — 6 points to distribute.
  4. Choose four skill triggers at +2. These describe what your pilot is good at outside the cockpit — and this campaign has a lot of outside-the-cockpit.
  5. Pick your background. See the Vareth-specific list below.
  6. Take your mech talents (three at rank 1) and pilot gear.
  7. Answer the six questions in § 03. This is the important bit.
A Note on Skill Triggers Roughly 40% of this campaign happens on foot or in conversation. Triggers like Get a Hold Of Someone, Investigate, Read a Situation, and Charm will get as much use as combat ones. Don't dump them.

OP.02 // ORIGINSWhy You're On Vareth

Nobody takes a Category III contract at the edge of charted space by accident. Pick one, or write your own.

The Debt

You owe someone — a corpro-state, a family, a hospital. Vareth's hazard pay is the fastest legal money in the sector. You are counting down.

The Washout

You had a career somewhere better. Something ended it — a court martial, a scandal, a mistake with a body count. Atlas doesn't check references as thoroughly as they should.

The Believer

You actually buy the pitch. Terraforming makes worlds liveable, someone has to do the dangerous part, and you'd rather it be someone competent. This is the most fragile origin and the most interesting.

The Legacy

Someone in your family fought in the Expansion Wars. You've read the records. Some of them don't make sense. You requested this posting specifically.

The Runner

You are here because 340 light-years is a good distance from a thing you did. Nobody on Vareth asks questions. That's the appeal.

The Company Kid

You were born into corporate housing on a corporate world, educated on corporate scrip. You've never known anything else. Vareth is your first posting where the company's story stops matching what you see.

OP.03 // INTEGRATIONThe Six Questions

Answer all six before session one. They exist so the campaign has things it can threaten, complicate, and cost you. Vague answers make for a vague character.

1 · A Person Someone you care about. On Vareth or back home. Name them. What do they want from you?
2 · A Place Somewhere at Site Zero you consider yours. A bunk, a corner of the Undertow, a spot on the perimeter wall. Where do you go when it's bad?
3 · A Responsibility Someone or something you're accountable for. A rookie you're mentoring. A debt. A promise. Something that gets harder to keep.
4 · A Goal What are you actually trying to get out of this posting? Be specific. "Survive" is not specific.
5 · A Problem Something that followed you here and hasn't finished with you.
6 · A Secret Something you haven't told the rest of the crew. It doesn't have to be dark. It has to be true, and it has to cost something if it comes out.

OP.04 // THREADSThree Ongoing Storylines

Every pilot carries three active threads. These get revisited during downtime and pulled into missions when they fit. Write one sentence for each — they can and should change as you play.

Thread One

Personal

Something about your life outside the job.

"I need to clear my sister's medical debt before her coverage lapses."

Thread Two

Professional

Something about your career or standing.

"I want Solberg to recommend me for a SPEC reclassification."

Thread Three

Mystery

Something tying you to the wider story.

"My grandfather's service record lists a posting that doesn't exist in any archive."

On the Mystery Thread This one is your handle on the campaign's larger story. It doesn't need to be dramatic — a name, a document, a recurring dream, an inherited object with no explanation. Give your GM something to build on and they will.

OP.05 // THE CREWParty Connections

Before session one, each player answers one question about the pilot to their left. Roll, pick, or invent:

  • We served together before Vareth. What went wrong?
  • They owe me. For what?
  • I owe them. What did it cost them?
  • I know something about them they haven't told the crew.
  • We were assigned as a pair on the transit out. What did we talk about?
  • I don't like them, and I'd still put myself between them and incoming fire. Why?
  • They remind me of someone. Who, and are they still alive?

OP.07 // CHECKLISTBefore Session One

  • Pilot built in COMP/CON at LL0, callsign chosen
  • Background picked from § 02 (or written)
  • All six integration questions answered in writing
  • Three threads written, one sentence each
  • One connection to another player's pilot
  • One thing your pilot is bad at, that isn't combat
