The World

Vareth is a marginal world at the edge of charted space. Atlas is here to make it liveable. Something on the ground disagrees.

OP.02 // HISTORYThe Official Version

Vareth was used during the First Corporate-Government Expansion Wars (2140 to 2158), a conflict most people remember as a footnote and nobody remembers fondly. Something was built here. It was abandoned when the war ended.

Under the peace settlement, Vareth was declared an exclusion site. Closed to settlement, salvage, and survey. It stayed closed for nearly four hundred years. In 2549 the charter governing that exclusion lapsed during a routine legislative consolidation. Atlas filed first and won the claim in 2550.

What Your Pilot Would Know Common knowledge: Vareth was a war site. It's been sealed a long time. Atlas got the rights through what everyone assumes was luck, lobbying, or both. The details of what was actually built here are not in any briefing packet you've been given.

OP.03 // TERRAINOperational Regions

Zone: Frontier · Active Operations

Ashfall Delta

Grey volcanic silt at a river mouth on the coast. The safest, lowest, most workable ground on the planet. This is why Site Zero is here. Fast-breeding scavenger fauna, mineral-heavy soil, and weather that turns without much warning.

Zone: Red · Restricted

The Glass Wastes

Kilometres of black-green glass, fused by something that happened here during the war. Unexploded ordnance. Live minefields. Geothermal vents. Atlas surveys it from a distance. Contract Security occasionally doesn't get that luxury.

Zone: Wild · Largely Unsurveyed

The Bloomlands

A dense forest belt growing faster and more aggressively than it has any right to. The flora here has developed defences that interfere with sensors and targeting systems. Nobody has satisfactorily explained why.

Zone: Wild · Barely Charted

The Choral Deep

A subterranean cave-and-fungal network beneath the Wastes. Named for the low resonant sound it makes when disturbed. Genuinely beautiful. Genuinely unnerving. Atlas has surveyed perhaps four percent of it.

Access Advisory A fifth region exists at the planet's mountainous headwaters. It does not appear on contractor-issue maps. Atlas classifies it as ████████████████ and lists it as closed to all personnel.

OP.03B // UNCLAIMEDBeyond the Survey Line

Four more regions show up on deep-orbital scans and nowhere else. No corporation has put a boot on the ground in any of them yet. On Vareth that doesn't mean safe. It just means nobody's found out why it isn't, yet. Field knowledge here is thin to nonexistent.

Zone: Wild · No Reports Filed

The Coast, South of the Delta

Shallow water, a lot of it. Survey drones sent past the river mouth have returned sonar readings nobody's explained. There's a rhythm to them, almost. Nobody's requested a coastal posting twice.

Zone: Wild · Unclaimed High Ground

The Western Peaks

Mountains west of the restricted zone. Cold, thin air, and, per the one long-range scan anyone's admitted to running, a clear line of sight onto whatever sits at the headwaters. Nobody's officially noticed that yet.

Zone: Effectively Sealed · Polar

The Northern Ice

Nobody's been. Nobody's asked to go. It's cold enough that Atlas hasn't bothered writing a hazard classification for it, which several contractors have pointed out is strange, given how thoroughly Atlas classifies everything else.

Zone: Wild · Verdance-Adjacent

The Eastern Caldera

An active volcanic field past the Wastes. Verdance apparently has people out there already, quietly, for reasons they haven't shared with anyone at Site Zero. Geothermal readings are supposedly excellent, if anyone ever gets around to a power contract.

Player Note Your pilot has never operated in any of these four regions. Neither has anyone Atlas has told you about. If your GM sends you to one, you're on genuinely first-footprint ground. Act like it.

OP.05 // RUMOURSWhat They Say at the Undertow

None of this is official. Some of it is nonsense. Your pilot has heard most of it.

  • The crew that worked this contract before you didn't all rotate home. The paperwork says otherwise.
  • There's an office in the east annex with no name on the door and no listing in the directory.
  • Somebody's buying salvage that nobody is officially selling.
  • Equipment failures on Contract Security frames run higher than on TerraOps kit. Nobody will log it.
  • A transport driver filed a sighting report last quarter. It came back reclassified as "wildlife," her name still on it.
  • Atlas isn't the only company on this rock. Nobody in Contract Security has been told who else is.
  • The wildlife out there doesn't behave like wildlife.
